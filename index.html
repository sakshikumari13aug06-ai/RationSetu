Design Thinking Specification Matrix

Structured mapping of the 3 fundamental prototype questions

1 User Interaction Point: Pre-Visit Smart Touchpoint

Where & When: The interaction happens at the beneficiary’s home or workplace before commuting to the Fair Price Shop.

The Context: Beneficiaries are wage-earners. Traveling 3 km and finding the shop shut or stock dry costs a day’s livelihood (₹500). By providing a 1-tap live status & reservation touchpoint, the app eliminates blind commutes.

Interaction Flow: Beneficiary opens App ➔ Views live grain inventory & queue wait ➔ Locks 20-min arrival window ➔ Receives offline QR token.
2 Single Element Addressing User's Difficulty: Guaranteed Stock & Fast-Pass Token Card

The Core Difficulty: "The Black Hole of Uncertainty & Wasted Work Hours." Standing in unorganized queues for 3–4 hours, facing frequent "stock finished" or "machine not working" excuses from dealers.

Why This Single Element: Rather than a bloated portal with complex menus, this single unified card directly resolves the pain point by: (a) Earmarking the beneficiary’s exact entitlement in the database, (b) Displaying live queue time (~12 mins), (c) Generating an offline cryptographically signed token that guarantees grain allocation.

3 What We Do Not Yet Know Will Work: Dealer Compliance & Queue Friction

The Critical Untested Assumption: Will Fair Price Shop dealers respect digital reservation tokens when pressured by an aggressive, non-digital crowd waiting physically outside in the heat?

How the Prototype Tests This:

  • Hybrid Queue Allocation (70% slots / 30% walk-ins) to ensure fairness for non-smartphone users.
  • Offline Cryptographic Verification so dealers cannot cite "internet connection down" to turn people away.
  • Geo-Verified Inspector SOS button providing direct accountability if a dealer refuses a valid digital token.

How to divide and present this Design Thinking project seamlessly

"We solved this by creating a Pre-Commute Smart Touchpoint. Before leaving home at 7:30 AM, Ramesh opens the app to see live shop status, wait time, and available grain balance."

Your Goal: Explain why a single unified card is better than 20 complex menus for low-digital-literacy citizens, and demonstrate the 24-hr stock lock.

"To answer the teacher's question on 'Which single element solves the difficulty?', we built the Guaranteed Stock & Fast-Pass Token Card. Instead of deep menus, this single hero card collapses 4 critical needs: live stock meters (20kg Rice, 10kg Wheat), crowd wait time, 24-hour stock lock, and an offline verifiable QR token."
